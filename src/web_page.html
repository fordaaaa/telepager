Connect Telegram

telepager pages you through your own Telegram bot. This takes about a minute, and nothing leaves your machine except messages to Telegram.

1 — bot token

Message @BotFather, send /newbot, and paste the token it gives you.

2 — your user id

Only these ids can talk to the bot. Open your bot in Telegram and send it anything, then press Detect — or type your id in by hand.

3 — save
